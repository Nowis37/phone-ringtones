A different ringtone for one contact on iPhone

Know who's calling without looking: every contact can have their own ringtone.

In short

Install the ringtone first, then open the contact's card: Edit › Ringtone, and pick it. Phone Ringtones opens the card for you.

What to know

iOS only lists installed ringtones in a contact's card. And no app can pick a contact's ringtone for you: you tap the last button yourself.

With Phone Ringtones

  1. Make your ringtone, then tap Assign to a contact and pick the contact.
  2. Tap Install and open the card: the ringtone installs, then the contact's card opens when you come back to the app.
  3. On the card, tap Edit, then Ringtone, and pick your new ringtone.
  4. Installing it also made it your main ringtone. To put the old one back for everyone else: Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone.

Without the app

In the Contacts app: pick the contact, tap Edit, then Ringtone. If your ringtone isn't there, it isn't installed yet.
