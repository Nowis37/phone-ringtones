A custom iPhone ringtone, without GarageBand

For years, putting your own ringtone on an iPhone meant GarageBand or a computer. Since iOS 26, it doesn't anymore.

In short

On iOS 26 or later, share a sound of 30 seconds or less and choose Use as Ringtone. Before iOS 26, without a computer, GarageBand is still the only way.

Check your iOS version

Go to Settings › General › About and look at the iOS version.

iOS 26 and later: one tap

The iPhone offers the Use as Ringtone action in the share sheet, for a sound of 30 seconds or less in a format it accepts. Phone Ringtones prepares the sound for you:

  1. Make your ringtone in Phone Ringtones, from a video, an audio file or one of our sounds.
  2. Tap Set ringtone, then Use as Ringtone.
  3. If Settings opens by itself with your ringtone selected, it's installed.

Before iOS 26

Without a computer, there is no other way than GarageBand, Apple's free app. Phone Ringtones takes you there and shows you the three steps. They're in Turn a video into an iPhone ringtone.
